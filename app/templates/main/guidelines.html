Community Guidelines

Please follow these simple rules to keep Volstruis Gids a safe and useful platform for everyone in the Klein Karoo.

Allowed

  • Respectful communication between users
  • Legal goods and services only
Not Allowed

  • Scams, fraud, or misleading listings

  • Hate speech or harassment
  • Adult content or illegal substances
